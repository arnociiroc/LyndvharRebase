<b>Lyndhardtia burns. An age of wealth and prosperity has been destroyed in just a decade. What will the future hold for all of Beowricke?</b>
<br>
<br>
Nearly ten years have passed since the beginning of the Ruby War, a civil war which has completely and utterly ravaged the once prosperous and powerful Lyndhardtian Empire. Once a dispute over the succession of the Ruby Throne has spiraled into a catastrophe the likes of which the realm had never seen before, resulting in its fracture and separation into warring states of warlords.
<br>
<br>
The most powerful of these warlords would emerge as four separate succession states, all vying for control of the once vast wealth of the Empire. And thus- the Lyndhardtian Empire’s two-hundred year history ended in all but ten years. What remains now is a cold, leftover memory of the power that once was creeping across a desolated region of the world.
<br>
<br>
The port of Lyndvhar is one of the last few bastions that has not completely collapsed to encroaching misery and destruction. Will it be able to weather the storm?
